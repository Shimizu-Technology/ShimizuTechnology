See how it connects · Illustrative workflow

From daily shift punches to archived payroll records.

Illustrative 4-step workflow showing how a Guam workforce time clock (such as AIRE Services) connects into a structured payroll run (such as Cornerstone Payroll).

  1. Step 01 · Time & attendance capture

    Staff clock in

    Employees clock in and out from a workplace kiosk or location-aware mobile clock-in, logging shifts, breaks, and work categories.

  2. Step 02 · Review & immutable cutoff

    Manager approves exceptions

    Supervisors review missed punches, overtime, leave, and adjustments, then finalize an immutable cutoff batch.

  3. Step 03 · Structured pay run

    Payroll calculates & commits

    Approved hours flow into hourly, salary, deduction, and Guam tax calculations. Accounting staff review totals before approving and committing the run.

  4. Step 04 · Checks, stubs & audit trail

    Checks & records archived

    Paper checks and earnings statements are prepared separately from issued and paid status, with audit history linked back to source entries.
