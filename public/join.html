Need to install or update the app?

Håfa Baby is an iPhone app in private testing through TestFlight.

  1. Get tester access

    Ask the person who invited you to have you added as a tester. Apple sends an email with access to Håfa Baby.

  2. Install Håfa Baby with TestFlight

    Get TestFlight, then follow Apple’s invitation email. Already a tester? Open TestFlight to install or update Håfa Baby.

    Get TestFlight

  3. Tap your family invitation again

    Return to the original message after installing. Tap its link, then Open Håfa Baby if this page appears.

Invitations work once, for 7 days. Keep yours between you and the person who invited you.
