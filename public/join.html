You’ve been invited to a family in Håfa Baby

If you’re seeing this page, Håfa Baby isn’t on this phone yet, or it needs an update. It’s in private testing, so it comes through TestFlight, Apple’s app for trying apps before they’re released. Testing is on iPhone for now.

  1. Ask to be added as a tester

    Ask the person who invited you. Apple sends the TestFlight invitation by email.

  2. Get TestFlight

    It’s free, from the App Store.

    Get TestFlight

  3. Install Håfa Baby

    Open Apple’s email and follow its link into TestFlight. Already have Håfa Baby? Open TestFlight and update it.

  4. Tap the link in your message again

    Håfa Baby opens with your invitation filled in. If you come back here instead, open Håfa Baby, then your profile, Family and I have a code, and paste the code from your message.

Nothing is shared until you’ve read what joining means and said yes. Your code works once, for 7 days, so keep it to yourself. This page doesn’t read it.
